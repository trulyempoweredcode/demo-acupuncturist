I treat a wide range of conditions using acupuncture, cupping, moxibustion, and traditional Chinese medicine techniques. Every treatment plan is personalised to your unique needs.

Pain Relief & Musculoskeletal

Pain is one of the most common reasons people seek acupuncture, and it's one of the areas where the evidence is strongest. Whether you're dealing with a long-standing condition or a recent injury, acupuncture can reduce inflammation, release tight muscles, and stimulate your body's natural painkillers.

I combine body acupuncture with trigger point needling and cupping where appropriate. Most patients notice a significant reduction in pain within 3-6 sessions, though some conditions respond even sooner.

  • Lower back pain and sciatica
  • Neck and shoulder tension
  • Migraines and tension headaches
  • Arthritis and joint pain
  • Sports injuries and RSI
  • Fibromyalgia

Women's Health

Traditional Chinese Medicine has a long history of supporting women's health through every stage of life. Acupuncture can help regulate hormones, ease menstrual discomfort, and support the body through menopause — all without the side effects of pharmaceutical treatments.

I take a gentle, holistic approach that considers your cycle, stress levels, sleep, and diet alongside your primary symptoms. Many women find that treatment improves not just the condition they came for, but their overall energy and wellbeing.

  • Painful or irregular periods
  • Endometriosis and PCOS
  • Menopause symptoms (hot flushes, mood, sleep)
  • PMS and hormonal imbalances
  • Pregnancy-related discomfort

Stress, Anxiety & Sleep

Modern life places enormous demands on our nervous systems. Acupuncture is deeply calming — it activates the parasympathetic nervous system, reduces cortisol levels, and helps your body shift out of a constant state of fight-or-flight.

Many of my patients describe feeling a profound sense of relaxation during and after treatment. With regular sessions, this translates into better sleep, lower anxiety, and an improved ability to cope with daily pressures.

  • Chronic stress and burnout
  • Generalised anxiety
  • Insomnia and poor sleep quality
  • Fatigue and low energy
  • Depression and emotional wellbeing

Digestive Health

In Chinese medicine, the digestive system is considered the foundation of good health. When digestion is strong, the whole body benefits. I frequently see patients with IBS, bloating, acid reflux, and other gut complaints that haven't responded well to conventional treatment.

Acupuncture helps regulate gut motility, reduce inflammation in the digestive tract, and calm the gut-brain axis. I combine needling with dietary advice based on Chinese medicine principles to help you restore comfortable, healthy digestion.

  • Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)
  • Bloating and indigestion
  • Acid reflux and GORD
  • Nausea (including morning sickness)
  • Constipation and diarrhoea

Fertility Support

Acupuncture can play a valuable role in supporting fertility, whether you're trying to conceive naturally or undergoing IVF or other assisted reproduction treatments. Research suggests acupuncture can improve blood flow to the reproductive organs, regulate hormones, and reduce the stress that can interfere with conception.

I hold a post-graduate diploma in fertility acupuncture and work closely with local fertility clinics. I can tailor treatment to your cycle and coordinate with your medical team if you're undergoing IVF.

  • Natural fertility enhancement
  • IVF and IUI support
  • Male fertility support
  • Cycle regulation
  • Pregnancy wellbeing and birth preparation

Cosmetic Acupuncture

Cosmetic acupuncture — sometimes called an acupuncture facelift — is a natural, non-invasive alternative to Botox and fillers. Fine needles are placed at specific facial points to increase collagen production, improve muscle tone, and enhance blood circulation to the skin.

A typical course involves 10 weekly sessions, after which most patients notice visibly firmer skin, reduced fine lines, and a brighter complexion. I also treat the body during each session to support overall health and address underlying imbalances that may be affecting your skin.

  • Fine lines and wrinkle reduction
  • Improved skin tone and texture
  • Natural collagen boost
  • Facial muscle toning and lifting
  • Acne and skin conditions
