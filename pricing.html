What's Included

Every treatment session includes a personalised consultation, pulse and tongue diagnosis, acupuncture treatment, and additional techniques (cupping, moxibustion, or electroacupuncture) where appropriate.

I also provide tailored dietary and lifestyle advice at no extra charge. You'll receive a written treatment plan after your initial consultation outlining the recommended course of treatment and expected outcomes.

Payment is accepted by card, bank transfer, or cash. Some private health insurance policies cover acupuncture — please check with your provider.
