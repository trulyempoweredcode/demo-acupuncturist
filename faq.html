New to acupuncture? Here are answers to the questions I'm asked most often. If yours isn't here, please get in touch.

Most people are surprised by how painless acupuncture is. The needles are extremely fine — much thinner than a hypodermic needle. You may feel a brief tingling or dull ache as the needle is inserted. Many patients find the experience deeply relaxing and even fall asleep during treatment.
